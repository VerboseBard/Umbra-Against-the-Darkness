Corrections applied to M01_mexico_history_1910_1940.md

All marked ⟨fixed: V_M01⟩ or [UNVERIFIED — V_M01] in the text; nothing else was changed.

  1. Gold Shirts — Senate "next day" → two days later (22 Nov); dissolution date (27 Feb 1936) and Rodríguez's Aug 1936 exile added.
  2. Cubilete — first stone 1920 (small monument) vs 11 Jan 1923 (monumental Christ, Filippi expelled); "dedicated in 1923" removed; dynamite/aircraft accounts kept with sources; height given as 20/23 m; "geographic centre" reworded to "traditionally called" in the entry, the overview, the odd detail, the hook and Top 10 #3.
  3. Torreón — the 30 million pesos is China's demand; Mexico granted 3.1 million; dead = 303 Chinese + 5 Japanese.
  4. Yaqui — "President-elect Obregón" → former president (WRONG); crowd size given as ~2,000 (TIME: 3,000+).
  5. Carranza — cause of death: murder is the usual account; suicide theory noted; the 4:30 a.m. time tagged UNVERIFIED (also in the heading).
  6. Naco — bombing dates corrected (battle 31 Mar; American side hit 2–6 Apr); Top 10 #8 relabelled.
  7. Sonora Chinese expulsion — the governor attribution and "about 3,500" replaced with the sourced sequence; 92% tagged.
  8. Small fixes/tags: Raúl Madero's age (3 or 4); Pact of the Embassy date (18–19 Feb); Veracruz priests "wounding two" (Vatican: one wounded, one unhurt); tags on Cerro Azul "16 miles/two-mile radius", the Vega nickname, Ramírez Frías, Islas Marías, the Coyoacán champagne (overview, entry, odd detail, hook), the Tampico Times "in ruins" line (and the hurricane date/toll note), and the Zalce print's date.
