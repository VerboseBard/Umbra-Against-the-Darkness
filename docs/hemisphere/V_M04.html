V_M04 — Fact-check of M04_haiti_dominican_republic.md

  • Checked: 2026-09-30, Claude Sonnet 5.5. No GPU, no git. Only M04_haiti_dominican_republic.md was edited (fixes marked ⟨fixed: V_M04⟩ / [UNVERIFIED — V_M04]); nothing else in the chapter was touched.
  • Method: the Top 10 first, then specific dates, numbers, names and quotes. I re-read primary text where it existed: Seabrook's The Magic Island (archive.org full text), Mars 1945 (JSTOR PDF, text extracted), Johnson's Self-Determining Haiti (Gutenberg) and the Senate committee's own hearing transcript (archive.org full text), plus Wikipedia, the Sciences Po and Bucknell pages, the Presidency Project and Wirkus/Trujillo/San Zenón/Parsley articles. A few rows rest on a WebSearch summary and say so.
  • Totals (claims checked: 55): CONFIRMED 48 · CORRECTED 2 · UNSUPPORTED 5 · WRONG 0. (CONFIRMED includes claims confirmed with a footnote; UNSUPPORTED = not found on any page read, now tagged in the chapter; CORRECTED = the chapter's wording or date differed from the source.)
  • Headline: the zombie material is as solid as the chapter says: the Hasco story and Article "249" are in Seabrook's own text, and Felix-Mentor is exactly as Mars printed it. The two weak points are the famous "200 Germans, 80% of the trade" (repeated everywhere, sourced nowhere I could find — now tagged) and a quotation attributed to Johnson that he did not write ("roped together"). The McCormick bridge to Chicago is stronger than the chapter says: the transcript shows him presiding in Port-au-Prince on 29 Nov 1921.
