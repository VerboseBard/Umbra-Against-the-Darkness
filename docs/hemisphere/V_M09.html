1 Veracruz customs money: 2,604,051.20 pesos = 1,539,051.20 in a sealed field safe + 1,065,000 in a sealed wooden box; Winship shipped 1 Jan 1915 to the New Orleans subtreasury; Funston reached Galveston 26 Nov 1914; funds first in First National Bank of Galveston; Garrison letter 26 Jan 1916 CONFIRMED FRUS 1916 d563 (read in full): "one field safe, sealed, containing 1,539,051.20 Mexican pesos, and the books and records pertaining to the military treasurer's office, together with one sealed wooden box containing 1,065,000 Mexican pesos, or a net total of 2,604,051.20 pesos"; orders to bring "all funds… both United States funds and Mexican custom receipts and taxes". https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1916/d563. D. What the owner should know before using M09

  • The two claims most likely to be repeated at the table are both sound: the 2,604,051.20-peso sealed safe and box and the Prieto terminal killings are straight from FRUS.
  • Do not repeat: "Paymaster Edgar House fired his shotgun" (FRUS: he refused to surrender it and was killed in the fusillade — no shot of his is mentioned); "83 arrested"; "Noguchi in Veracruz"; "de la Huerta to Mérida"; "second-busiest oil port".
  • The tenant-strike casualty story is real but its details vary by source: 4 dead/22 wounded (official), 150 (union, 1926); bodies at Playa Norte and Villa del Mar; a clandestine burial at the Inalámbrica-station garrison; Col. Aarón López Manzano as the ordering officer.
