T1a Our Lady of Charity declared patroness of Cuba, 10 May 1916 (Benedict XV) CONFIRMED "Pope Benedict XV declared Our Lady of Charity of El Cobre Patroness of Cuba via a decree signed on 10 May 1916" (petition letter of 24 Sept 1915). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Charity
T1b Canonically crowned 20 Dec 1936 at Santiago during the Eucharistic Congress CONFIRMED Same page: Pius XI granted the canonical coronation on 20 December 1936, at Santiago de Cuba, performed by Mgr Valentín Zubizarreta y Unamunsaga. (The "three Juans"/1612/Nipe legend detail was not on that page — not checked further.)
T1c 20 Dec = two days before the solstice, eight before the eclipse; San Lázaro 17 Dec CONFIRMED Arithmetic on the owner's alpha calendar; 17 Dec 1917 first Rincón pilgrimage confirmed (T-row 34).
T2a Wood wrote "little or no independence left Cuba under the Platt Amendment" CONFIRMED (quote), UNSUPPORTED (date, addressee page) Quote read on https://peacehistory-usfp.org/yankee-imperialism/ ("little or no independence left in Cuba under the Platt Amendment" — wording varies by reproduction) and in search summaries quoting the Roosevelt Center. The Theodore Roosevelt Center page the chapter cites does not contain the letter (fetched: no such text). Date "October 1901" not found.
T2b Wood's governorship 23 Dec 1899 – 20 May 1902; Chinese exclusion order 15 May 1902 CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Wood
T2c The gambling licence: Frontón opened 7 May 1901; Col. Edward S. Dudley refused three times; Wood backed it; ran unlicensed until a ten-year contract in 1904; "Palacio de los Gritos"/"El Matadero" CONFIRMED https://abandonedfl.com/fronton-jai-alai/ (read) and a WebSearch summary of the other Havana-fronton pages. Local-history source, Medium as the chapter says.
T3 1932 ABC cemetery tunnel plot (Vázquez Bello; tunnel; 200 lb dynamite; 700 m; family buried him in Santa Clara; Freyre de Andrade reprisals) CORRECTED Killing date is 27 or 28 Sept 1932 (Wikipedia EN/ES and Cuba Memorias: 28th; OnCuba "martes 27 de septiembre" and Fotos de La Habana: 27th). The tunnel (~200 m, along the cemetery's drain network) ran to the Truffin family pantheon, not "the Vázquez Bello vault"; dynamite: "cuatro fardos… 200 libras" (OnCuba) or 259 lb + 134 cheddite cartridges (Fotos de La Habana); 700 m was the wire, not the tunnel. Buried Santa Clara 29 Sept (widow abroad telegraphed). Reprisal killing of three Freyre de Andrade brothers CONFIRMED (Wikipedia). Fotos de La Habana says instant death; Wikipedia says operating table. https://oncubanews.com/cuba/sociedad-cuba/historia/el-atentado-a-vazquez-bello/ ; https://www.fotosdlahabana.com/atentado-muerte-clemente-vazquez-bello/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemente_Vazquez_Bello
T3b ABC founded Oct 1931 by Martínez Sáenz, with Mañach and Ichaso; ~7-member cells; green banner CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABC_(Cuba)
T4 1912 massacre: rising 20 May 1912; 3,000–6,000 killed; Marines 28 May (32 officers, 777 men) and 7 June; Estenoz killed 27 June; Ivonnet killed 18 July "while trying to escape"; last Marines left 2 Aug CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negro_Rebellion (redirect to "War of 1912"). The specific 1,292 Marines on 7 June is not on the page (UNSUPPORTED, tagged).
T4b Helg: "entire families were machine-gunned in their bohíos" CONFIRMED Quoted in a WebSearch result for Our Rightful Share (page itself not fetched).
T5 Coolidge in Havana 15–17 Jan 1928; USS Texas; Sixth Pan-American Conference 16 Jan–20 Feb; only foreign trip; Hughes, Morrow, Mencken, Will Rogers CONFIRMED https://history.state.gov/departmenthistory/travels/president/coolidge-calvin (15–17 Jan 1928, addressed the Sixth Conference); ship, train to Key West, companions and "only foreign trip" from WebSearch summaries (Coolidge Foundation, USNI). Divergence call (Coolidge cannot happen; Dawes is president) rests on the setting, not on this check.
T6 Lansky's arrival date contested CONFIRMED (as contested) Wikipedia's Oriental Park page: "In 1937, gangster Meyer Lansky gained control of the racetrack and casino"; Cigar Aficionado (read): "in 1939, Batista recruited… Lou Smith… and Meyer Lansky to clean up the casinos". T7 Maine: 261 of 355; 21:40; 66 remains recovered in 1911, one identified; scuttled 16 Mar 1912 in 3,600 ft; Rickover 1976 CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Maine_(1889) (the remainder were reburied at Arlington).
T8 Machado's fall 12 Aug 1933: left 3:32 p.m., four aides, Nassau, £500,000/500,000 gold dollars, wife to Key West, "I've got Machado's sheets", Jiménez shot by Lt. Pérez Villalón, 50 porristas killed, Souto allowed to shoot himself CONFIRMED TIME, "Cuba: Loot the Palace!" https://time.com/archive/6753146/cuba-loot-the-palace/ — see the two small corrections in the table below (destroyers; the garden).
T9 Sergeants' Revolt 4 Sept 1933; Pentarchy five days; Grau's Hundred Days; fell Jan 1934; Hotel Nacional siege 2 Oct 1933 CORRECTED (officer count) / CONFIRMED (rest) Revolt facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_Revolution_of_1933. Officers besieged: about 400, not 300 (Wikipedia battle article and revolution article); ~94 dead (~80 soldiers + 14 officers) CONFIRMED; post-surrender murders now CONFIRMED (Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hotel_Nacional_of_Cuba
T10 Chicago–Havana threads: Curly Brown/Arlington Park ran Oriental Park; Capone at the Sevilla; Crowder died a Chicago lawyer UNSUPPORTED (Capone years) / CONFIRMED (rest) Brown and Bowman as Havana-American Jockey Club presidents CONFIRMED (Oriental Park page).
