Mexico, Cuba and the Caribbean, 1898–1940 — Umbra research library (sweep of 2026-09-30)

Real history for UMBRA's later books — Mexico (the setting's Southern War, the ceded north, the Tampico enclave, Veracruz's customs house), Cuba (the centre of the Golden Circle's map), Haiti, the Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Nicaragua, the Canal Zone and the European colonies. Gathered overnight on 2026-09-30 on the owner's instruction (relayed from his video session): "research Mexico and the areas [already discussed]: the Caribbean islands and Cuba … folklore that fits Umbra, murders, events, deaths, destruction of old churches."

Nothing here is canon. Nothing was merged into the rulebook or Docs/lore/ALTERNATE_HISTORY.md. Every hook is a proposal. Facts carry source URLs; ⟨fixed: V_Mnn⟩ = corrected by a fact-checker; [UNVERIFIED …] = not confirmed. BRIEF.md is the instruction every agent worked from (it points to the setting's own hemisphere sources). The Chicago sweep's ..\2026-09-26_chicago_great_lakes\BRIEF.md supplied the entry format and rules.
