R05 A Czech and Slovak memorial turns Cermaks life into a drama

March 1933; reported March 24 as the previous Sunday | documented | Politics and civic life

After Mayor Anton Cermak died in Miami on March 6, Chicago's mourning continued beyond the main funeral. A March 24 Osadne Hlasy report describes Czech and Slovak organizations assembling at Harrison Auditorium the previous Sunday. They displayed a draped bust and a large symbolic book, heard speeches and music, and watched a dramatic portrayal of Cermak's life. This smaller commemorative event offers a specific neighborhood gathering, named speakers and stage imagery that a general mayoral biography misses.

Uncertainties

The source is an online English transcription or translation, not a checked original page. The publication date is exact; the event date is given relatively. The venue's street address remains unverified. The assassination happened in Miami, not Chicago.

Fictional Umbra possibility

Fictional possibility: a stage prop or memorial book has been substituted. The performers and organizers know who handled it, but each remembers a different last rehearsal.
