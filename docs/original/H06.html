H06 Coal delivery workers strike during winter

Reported from February 20 through at least mid-March 1935; end date not established | documented | Labor and fuel supply

A February 21 Daily Worker dispatch reports that nearly 3,000 Chicago coal delivery workers had struck the previous day, seeking a daily wage increase from six to seven dollars. Its account describes police mobilization, trucks stopped and coal dumped in streets. A March 15 issue still includes coal delivery workers among current Chicago strikes. These are contemporary but partisan reports. The episode offers a direct connection between wages, neighborhood coal yards and household fuel access without assuming that any particular district lost heat.

Uncertainties

Strike size, dumped tonnage and police claims need independent corroboration. Date_end records the latest report inspected, not settlement. No specific home-heating outage is established.
