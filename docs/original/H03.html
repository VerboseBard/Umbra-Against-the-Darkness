H03 The burned Stock Yards elevated branch remains severed for months

May 19, 1934–January 16, 1935 | documented | Transport and industrial life

The 1934 fire burned Halsted station, trapped a two-car train, and divided the Stock Yards elevated branch. Local transit-history specialist Chicago-L.org records that trains did not resume west of Halsted and around the Packingtown loop until January 16, 1935. Reconstruction was delayed by disagreement among the railway operator, property owner and packing companies over who should pay. A single disaster therefore changed workers' daily routes for roughly eight months, while damaged steel and contested responsibility remained visible reminders.
