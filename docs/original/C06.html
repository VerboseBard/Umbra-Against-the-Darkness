C06 Resurrection Mary: a period-set legend with later evidence

Legend commonly set in the late 1920s–1930s; earliest attestation not established | folklore | Folklore — not verified supernatural history

Resurrection Mary is a hitchhiking-ghost story associated with Archer Avenue, Resurrection Cemetery and the Oh Henry Ballroom, later called Willowbrook Ballroom. In common versions, a young woman in a white dress dances, accepts a ride and vanishes near the cemetery. The Chicago History Museum notes that stories place her death in the late 1920s or early 1930s but disagree about the circumstances and identity. This is strong evidence for a Chicago-area legend, not evidence that a supernatural event occurred or that the complete modern story circulated during the campaign years.
