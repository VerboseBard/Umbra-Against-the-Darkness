W12 Adler opens a mechanical sky over the lakefront

12 May 1930 opening; 1933–34 fair attendance | documented | Science, philanthropy and public space

The Adler Planetarium opened on 12 May 1930 as the first planetarium in the western hemisphere. Businessman Max Adler funded construction, a Zeiss projector and a collection of historic astronomical instruments, while the South Park Commissioners undertook operating costs. Philip Fox served as its first director. During the two fair seasons of 1933 and 1934 it received more than 1.2 million visits according to the institution’s history. For a campaign it provides a public scientific spectacle, a mechanical imitation of the heavens, and an authentic institutional network connecting private philanthropy to park government and fair organizers.
