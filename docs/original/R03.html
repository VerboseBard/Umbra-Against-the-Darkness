R03 Jane Addams funeral fills the Hull House courtyard

May 23 1935; Addams died May 21 | documented | Politics and civic life

Following Jane Addams's death on May 21, 1935, Chicago residents and visiting dignitaries crowded Hull-House's courtyard for her funeral on May 23. The gathering brought the settlement's neighborhood relationships into public view. Addams had received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1931, but Hull-House's local significance extended through education, social services and reform networks. A scene here can bring together immigrant families, settlement workers and prominent mourners. Most of the larger complex was destroyed decades later, in 1963; that demolition does not belong in the campaign's historical window.
