C07 Gospel crosses from storefronts into great churches

1931–1933 | documented | Religion and music

Thomas Dorsey's gospel music joined blues techniques to religious texts and met resistance from ministers who associated its sound with Saturday-night entertainment. A gospel chorus at Ebenezer in 1931 helped change that reception. Pilgrim pastor Junius Austin invited Dorsey to organize a choir at his church, and new gospel choruses spread during 1932. After his wife and infant died that year, Dorsey wrote Take My Hand, Precious Lord. Pilgrim occupied an Adler and Sullivan building formerly used as a synagogue. The story brings together musical innovation, bereavement, migration, architectural reuse and disagreement inside religious communities.
