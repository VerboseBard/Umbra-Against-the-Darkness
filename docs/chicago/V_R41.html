V_R41 — Fact-check of R41 (Indiana and Michigan beyond Chicago and Detroit)

Checker: Sonnet 5.5 · Date: 2026-09-30 · Method: every "Top 10" item first, then the numbers, dates, names and quotations in the best-known entries, each re-checked against the pages the entry cites (fetched this session; where a cited page could not be fetched I used the search-result text and say so). No GPU, no git; only R41 was edited.

Divergence labels flagged

  • Kingsford was labelled "holds — domestic calendar" but includes a Second World War aside (4,190 Waco CG-4 gliders); the setting has no Nazi Germany or U.S. war in Europe. Light BENDS added.
  • The chapter's other BENDS calls (Deutsche Haus, Nieuwland, Terre Haute's "Kerensky," Fountain Street's WWI memorial tower, Bay City's Salzburg, Hancock, South Bend's Polish parishes) checked out as correctly flagged; the Fountain Street tower claim itself was not re-verified.
  • Nothing labelled "holds" was found to touch the presidents after Wilson: Duvall (1926–31), Dale's Christmas-Eve 1933 pardon by Roosevelt, Murphy's 1937 governorship and the 1931 French Lick governors' conference all sit in years where the setting's presidents (Dawes to 1929, Hoover to 1933, Roosevelt after) agree with the record.
