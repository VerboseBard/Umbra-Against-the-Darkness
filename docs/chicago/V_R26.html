Divergence-label audit

Scanned every "Divergence check" line in R26 that touches the Great War, Germany, the USSR/communism, the presidents after Wilson, foreign policy, or empire. None were mislabeled "holds" — R26 is careful here and correctly marks BENDS on: the Valentiner General Staff service, the Order of the German Eagle being a Nazi decoration, the UAW's "Communist" framing, Reuther's Gorky years, Poland/Hamtramck, Windsor/Canada, and the Ford Hunger March's Communist organizers. No line merges the Secret Service with FBI Section 13 (the one mention of both, in the McCrea entry, correctly treats them as separate rival options).
