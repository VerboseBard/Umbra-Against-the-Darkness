V_R15 — Fact-check of R15 (Immigrant and Ethnic Chicago in the 1930s)

  • Checked by: Claude Fable 5.1 (cross-model fact-checker)
  • Date: 2026-09-26
  • Chapter under review: R15_ethnic_immigrant_chicago.md (Claude Opus 5.5, 64 entries)
  • Method: WebSearch was exhausted, so every check is a WebFetch re-opening of the chapter's own cited URL (FLPS item pages at flps.newberry.org, Wikipedia, Gutenberg, 3750lsd.net, chicagohistory.org, WBEZ), plus the brief's search endpoints (FLPS keyword search, Wikipedia search API, archive.org, LOC Chronicling America) where a cited page did not settle a claim. About 95 pages were opened. LOC Chronicling America and Google Books answered HTTP 429 for most of the session; HathiTrust 403; the Encyclopedia of Chicago host does not resolve (DNS), as the chapter itself reports.
  • Verdict key: CONFIRMED = the cited (or a substitute) source says it · CORRECTED = fact adjusted, right fact and URL given · UNSUPPORTED = not found in any page read; may still be true · WRONG = contradicted by the source.

Summary

127 claims checked across 119 table rows: 113 CONFIRMED · 4 CORRECTED · 6 UNSUPPORTED · 4 WRONG. Hard-error rate (CORRECTED + WRONG) = 8/127 = 6.3 %; counting UNSUPPORTED as well = 14/127 = 11.0 %.

The chapter is unusually faithful to its sources: every FLPS quotation sampled (about 45 items, including all ten "Top 10" entries) is on the cited page with the wording, names, addresses and figures as given. The errors are concentrated in glosses and inferences the author added around the sources (an etymology, an "only", a "ran"), one impossible date copied from a mis-dated FLPS card, and two mistyped URLs.

Worst three:

  1. "Justice for Hungary" dated 13 Apr 1930 (entry H, Hungarian) — the FLPS card carries that date and says the plane "recently made a trans-Atlantic flight", but the Endresz–Magyar crossing was 15–16 July 1931; the card is mis-dated. Otthon of 6 Dec 1931 (FLPS 5422061_2_1672) announces Magyar's post-flight reception at the Sherman Hotel with Mayor Cermak. Fixed.
  2. Sitch glossed as "Siege" (entry H, Ukrainian; Top 10 #1) — no such gloss in the source; Sich is the Zaporozhian Cossack stronghold. Fixed.
  3. Two dead Lithuanian URLs (4223970_… for 5423970_…) and Holy Trinity Orthodox called Sullivan's "only church" (it is one of two). Fixed.
