V_R10 — Fact-check of R10 (Secret societies, fringe politics, hate groups, plots & foreign intrigue)

  • Checked by: Claude Fable 5.1 (cross-model verifier), 2026-09-26
  • Chapter: R10_secret_societies_fringe_intrigue.md (62 entries, written by Claude Opus 5.5)
  • Method: WebSearch was exhausted for the session, so every claim was checked by WebFetch against the page the chapter cites (Wikipedia, Chicagology, ACWS, Underground History, the 1936 Spivak reprint, Civil War Months, Ohio Civil War Central, Cornell LII) plus independent pages (Handbook of Texas, U.S. Dept of Veterans Affairs, Wikipedia search API). 104 claims/claim-groups checked, covering all 62 entries' load-bearing facts, the whole "Top 10 for the owner", the divergence labels, 15 URLs and the "canon" rule.
  • Bottom line: the chapter is unusually accurate. 86 of 104 checked claims confirmed against their sources; 4 wrong, 9 corrected (5 substantive, 4 source-note fixes), 5 unsupported. The one error that matters for the setting is the Golden Circle's size (see §3).

3. The Golden Circle size — answer for the owner

The sources support a radius of 16 degrees ≈ 1,100–1,200 miles, i.e. a circle about 2,400 miles across (diameter), centred on Havana.

  • Handbook of Texas (TSHA): "Centering on Havana, this empire would be some 2,400 miles in diameter."
  • Wikipedia: "a circle of 16 degrees radius (about 2400 miles)" — the parenthesis is the diameter, not the radius (16° of latitude is ~1,100 mi). A 2,400-mile radius from Havana would reach Hudson Bay and Alaska, which no source claims; a 16° radius reaches roughly from Panama/northern Colombia to the Ohio River and Washington, D.C., which is exactly the territory every source lists.
  • So: R10's entry text and Top-10 line were wrong (they called 2,400 miles a radius) — fixed. The setting's own lore ("~1,200 miles wide") is also off by the same confusion in the other direction: 1,200 miles is the radius; the circle is ~2,400 miles wide. Recommend the owner's lore read "about 1,200 miles in every direction from Havana (2,400 miles across)".
