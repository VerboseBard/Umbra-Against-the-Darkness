Divergence-label audit

Checked every "Divergence check" line for a mislabeled "holds" that actually touches the Great War, Germany, the USSR/communism, the presidents after Wilson, foreign policy or empire. None found — R30 is careful here: it correctly marks BENDS on the Welland Canal's 1916–19 war-labour stoppage, the Seeandbee's 1942 Navy conversion, the Naval Training Station's entire WWI-era expansion ("BENDS hard"), the Norwegian Viator (Europe's shipping under a German peace), the "lake never gives up its dead" saying's link to French WWI minesweepers, and the 1940 Armistice Day storm (no Armistice Day in the setting). No line merges the Secret Service with FBI Section 13 — the file opens with an explicit coordinator's note stating they are separate, and every hook in the file that names both treats them as rivals who "do not tell each other."
