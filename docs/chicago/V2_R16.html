Coordinator correction (Section 13 vs. the Secret Service)

The coordinator's mid-run note says Section 13 (the FBI, directed by K. Edgar Hoover) and the Secret Service are separate, rival factions. I searched R16 for every mention of Section 13, the Secret Service, "the Service", Hoover and the FBI:

  • Fixed (#122): the Tracks/O'Hare hook made Section 13 the agency that "took" Capone and then called O'Hare "the Service's asset". It is now the Secret Service throughout, which matches the brief's own line that the Secret Service took Capone. It also carries a real detail from the O'Hare page: O'Hare's IRS contact, Frank J. Wilson, was Chief of the U.S. Secret Service from 1937 to 1946.
  • Checked, left alone:
    • The Section 13-only hooks (Green Mill, called shot, Arch Ward, Dill Pickle, Bronzeville balls, Swing Mikado, Blackstone) and the Secret Service-only hook (1933 title game) don't merge the two.
    • The tracks divergence line quotes the brief ("the Secret Service knew what Capone was…") correctly.
    • "Hoover" in the Chicago Stadium and Drake entries means Herbert Hoover.
    • One judgement call for the owner: the called-shot hook puts "Section 13 … in the box behind Roosevelt". Guarding a candidate is the Secret Service's historical job, but as written it's a surveillance proposal, not a conflation.
