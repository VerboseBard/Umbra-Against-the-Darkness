# Claim (R43 entry) Verdict Evidence / correction
1 The Knights of the Golden Circle's first "castle" was in Cincinnati, 1854, organised by G. W. L. Bickley CONFIRMED (contested origin) Wikipedia: Bickley founded "the first castle, or local branch, in Cincinnati in 1854"; the same paragraph notes the 1860 convention records say it "originated at Lexington, Kentucky, on the fourth day of July 1854" — R43 already carries that caveat. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Golden_Circle ; Vallandigham's page also says the KGC "had been founded in Cincinnati in 1854".
1b Bickley taught at the Eclectic Medical Institute on a falsified University of London degree; born 1823, d. 1867; left Cincinnati "hounded by creditors" in the late 1850s CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._L._Bickley (moved to Cincinnati 1851; "Professor of Materia Medica…" at the Eclectic Medical Institute, "by claiming to have been a graduate … University of London"; 18 July 1823 – 10 Aug 1867).
1c Circle of "16 degrees radius (about 2,400 miles)" on Havana, "25 new slave states"; R43's aside that 2,400 reads as a diameter CONFIRMED KGC page uses exactly "16 degrees radius (about 2400 miles)" and "25 new slave states"; R43's arithmetic aside is sound (16° ≈ 1,100 mi).
1d Order re-formed Feb 1864 as Sons of Liberty, Vallandigham Supreme Grand Commander; Vallandigham b. 29 July 1820, arrested 5 May 1863 under General Order 38, d. 17 June 1871 at Lebanon CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clement_Vallandigham (the KGC page says Feb 1864; the Vallandigham page says he became a leader "sometime around March 1864" in Canada — a month's slack).
1e Vallandigham died "demonstrating in court" how the victim might have shot himself CORRECTED He shot himself in his hotel room (the Lebanon House, later the Golden Lamb Inn) while showing fellow defence lawyers how he would demonstrate it to the jury; he died next day of peritonitis; client Thomas McGehean was acquitted. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clement_Vallandigham — fixed in the entry and in the "odd detail".
1f The Sandusky plot was "the only recorded Golden Circle raid on the lakes" / "its only recorded raid" / Top-10 "a Lake Erie warship raid in 1864" CORRECTED Wikipedia's Johnson's Island page calls it "a Confederate espionage ring that had conspired to seize the Great Lakes warship USS Michigan"; the KGC page says federal agents charged the Sons of Liberty with "an attempt to capture a Union warship on Lake Erie". So: a Confederate operation federal agents tied to the Golden Circle's successor order, not a Golden Circle raid. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson's_Island — overview, hook and Top-10 reworded (the entry body already said it right).
1g Johnson's Island: 300 acres, 3 miles off Sandusky, 16.5-acre prison, 15-ft stockade, 15,000+ men, ~200 died, 206 buried; cemetery bought by the UDC 1908; Ezekiel's statue 1910; NHL 1990 CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson's_Island (statue unveiled 8 June 1910; cemetery bought by the United Daughters of the Confederacy of Cincinnati).
2 Ohio Penitentiary fire, Easter Monday 21 April 1930: 322 dead, ~230 hospitalised, West Block, candle on oily rags, 500 soldiers from Fort Hayes with machine guns, Raymond/Gibbons/Grate blamed CONFIRMED https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_Penitentiary ("1930 fire" section — the separate "Ohio Penitentiary fire" article redirects there); Easter Sunday 1930 was 20 April. Population "4,300 … built to hold 1,500" and Himes's "To What Red Hell": https://www.columbusnavigator.com/ohio-penitentiary-fire-1930/ . Injured 230 (Wikipedia) vs ~130 (History.com, via usdeadlyevents.com) — R43 carries both.
2b "The keys were 900 feet from the locks" (odd detail, overview, "keys kept 900 feet away") CORRECTED The one page that states it says the keys "were kept 900 feet from where the fire started, but were missing when guards went to get them" — not 900 ft from the locks. https://www.mariettatimes.com/opinion/local-columns/2026/04/the-way-i-see-it-fire-devastated-ohio-pen-in-1930/ (quoting the 1930 press). The figure is absent from Wikipedia, Columbus Navigator, the Zinn Education page and usdeadlyevents.com, so it rests on that one column. Three places reworded.
2c Unclaimed dead buried five days later, whites at Eastlawn, Black inmates at Evergreen Cemetery "a mile away" CONFIRMED Same Marietta Times page, near-verbatim. (Single source; the search engine returns the same sentence.)
2d Hal Griswold's "Where were the keys to those cells?"; Gov. Myers Cooper called for Warden Thomas's suspension; Company A, 166th Infantry, 45 men from Marietta called by the fire bell on 29 April CONFIRMED Same Marietta Times page (it spells "Griswald"; the bell "rang 14 times at 11 a.m."). Prisoners refusing hard labour two weeks: https://www.zinnedproject.org/news/tdih/ohio-state-penitentiary-fire/ .
2e 315 men in the electric chair 1897–1963 CONFIRMED Wikipedia "Ohio Penitentiary": "312 men and three women were electrocuted between 1897 and 1963" (343 executions in all incl. 28 hanged).
2f The cell block beside the fire held "about 800, most already locked in for the night" UNSUPPORTED Not on any page read. Tagged.
3 Remus, 6 Oct 1927, Eden Park: "Run them down if you have to"; "Lock me up. I've just shot my wife."; jury out 19 minutes; juror "persecuted enough"; Judge Lueders "insane and a dangerous person to be at large"; Court of Appeals 2–1, 30 March 1928; Ohio Supreme Court 4–3, 19 June 1928; "Johnny Appleseed of Lima State Hospital" / Remus Grove CONFIRMED https://www.americanheritage.com/rise-and-fall-bootleg-king (all quotations, both votes and both dates); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Remus ("The jury deliberated only nineteen minutes"; Taft II prosecuting; stepdaughter Ruth — R43's "medium" hedge on the name is resolved; $40 million and ~3,000 employees; "80 percent of America's bonded whiskey" within 300 miles; New Year's Eve 1922 party for 100 couples; convicted 1925, two years in Atlanta).

The figure looks like a mix-up; replaced by the sourced description.

Bottom line

R43's Top-10 is sound on the big facts — the 1854 Cincinnati castle, the 322 dead and the segregated burial, Remus's 19 minutes and 4–3, Niles's Flaming Circle and martial law, Mellett, the Flyer in London, Harding's tomb and Phillips, Serpent Mound's solstice tail and WLW's 500 kW. The errors were: "keys 900 feet from the locks" (the source says 900 ft from where the fire started, and it is a single-source figure), "1,700 armed men" at Niles (wrong — that is the Mass crowd; no count exists), Vallandigham's fatal "demonstration" was in a hotel room, and the Sandusky plot was Confederate, tied by federal agents to the Golden Circle's successor — not a "Golden Circle raid". UNVERIFIED tags: the Klan's Ohio totals, Effinger's Toledo death, East Youngstown casualty counts, the NFL attendee list, the Abbot label condition and the Harold Madison identification.
