V_R35 — Fact-check of R35_catholic_chicago_ihs_ground.md

Fact-checker pass, 2026-09-27. Method: fetched the cited primary sources directly — the two Official Catholic Directory full texts on archive.org (downloaded and grepped the actual 1931/1938 OCR line-by-line rather than trusting a summary), the Mulkerins 1923 Holy Family Parish full text on archive.org, Wikipedia, and the parish/order/history pages the chapter cites (Case Western's Encyclopedia of Cleveland History, saintodilo.org, oladyf.weebly.com, sheffieldneighborhood.org). loc.gov was not needed (R35 cites no loc.gov URLs).
