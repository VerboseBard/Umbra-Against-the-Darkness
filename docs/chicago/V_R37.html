V_R37 — Fact-check of R37 (Gary and the Calumet Region)

Checker: Sonnet 5.5 · Date: 2026-09-30 · Method: every "Top 10" item first, then the numbers, dates, names and quotations in the best-known entries, each re-checked against the pages the entry cites (fetched this session). Where an entry rested on a "search-result summary only," I tried a real page. No GPU, no git; only R37 was edited.
