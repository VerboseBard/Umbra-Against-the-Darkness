"Holds" labels that touch WWI, Germany, the presidents after Wilson or the empire

  • Fulton Sheen — labelled "holds." His Louvain (Belgium) doctorate of 1923 and Roman studies touch Belgium's post-1914 status and Rome–Berlin relations in a German-won world. Flag added to the divergence line (BENDS lightly for any Louvain/Rome detail).
  • Springfield Klan (1921–27) — "holds," and the Klan's own 1915 revival predates the break. The 1924 "Ellis Island" float protests the immigration laws that in the real record were signed by Harding/Coolidge; in the setting (Wood 1921–27) the laws' authorship changes. Not edited; noted here.
  • Aurora "El Camp" — already BENDS; the "overseas immigration was restricted" line is the same Harding/Coolidge-era legislation, so the substitute needs a Wood-era cause.
  • Waukegan Sherman House (1920) — "holds," but the mob is sailors and Marines from Great Lakes Naval Training Station; the station's wartime expansion never happened in the setting, so the numbers ("hundreds") are a GM choice. Not edited.
  • Hoover, Roosevelt and Prohibition repeal appearances (Peoria 1932 visit; Lincoln Tomb rededication 17 Jun 1931; Hiram Walker/repeal 5 Dec 1933; Simmons Island New Deal relief) are all inside the canon's Hoover 1929–33 / Roosevelt 1933– calendar; no flag needed.
  • Already BENDS and correctly labelled: Palmer Raid, Camp Grant, Racine "Little Moscow," Aurora "El Camp," Moline's Belgians, Joliet's Slovenian parish, Danville National Home, Rockford's mustard-gas legend.
