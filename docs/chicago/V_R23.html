Divergence problems

  1. "Pardoned by Harding" (Wehde entry and Top-10 #3) — BENDS, unflagged. Harding is never president in the setting (Leonard Wood 1921–27). Fixed in R23 with a BENDS note.
  2. "No American entry, so no Leavenworth" (Wehde divergence line) is shaky. Wehde's offence was violating U.S. neutrality laws — a peacetime statute that a neutral America enforced in 1915 (it seized the Annie Larsen that June, while still neutral). A neutral setting-America could still prosecute the Siam gun-runners. Whether it would is the owner's call; the chapter should not present it as automatic. Noted here, not edited in R23 (it is a proposal, not a claim of fact).
  3. Everything else holds together. The Secret Service and FBI Section 13 are kept separate in every hook that names them (lines 220, 230, 523, 629). No hook calls itself canon; the word appears only in the required "(proposal, not canon)" tag. The Kaiser's-birthday-1916, Frantzius (d. Jan 1917) and APL/Palmer-raids divergence lines are consistent with the 1916 break and the setting's "no Red Scare".
