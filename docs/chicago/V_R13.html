Divergence-label problems

  1. Olmstead / wiretaps — marked "holds", should be BENDS. The 5–4 majority was Taft, Van Devanter, McReynolds, Sutherland, Sanford (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olmstead_v._United_States). Taft (1921), Sutherland (1922) and Sanford (1923) were Harding appointees (checker's general knowledge, not fetched) — and Harding is never president in the setting. The Court that decided Olmstead was appointed by President Wood; the ruling is an owner's choice, not a given.
  2. Brinkley — BENDS given only for the Silver Legion; misses the big one. XER/XERA broadcast from Villa Acuña, Coahuila, because Mexico was a sovereign country beyond the FRC. In the setting the US won the Southern War, took the mineral north and occupies Mexico: a "border blaster" out of US reach cannot exist as written. Empire/foreign policy — needs its own BENDS line.
  3. Insull — "holds" with a parenthetical "BENDS lightly". His flight to Greece and arrest in Turkey (1934) is a foreign-policy act; in the setting the Ottoman Empire survives and leans German. Label should be BENDS.
  4. Stratosphere balloons — "holds", but the note cites the unrecognised Soviet USSR-1 flight. No Soviet Union in the setting: the record context changes. Minor BENDS.
  5. Blood bank — "holds" + "minor BEND" note. Fantus's sources were Russian (Soviet) blood-storage work and the Spanish Civil War — both foreign-policy/Russia items. Label should be BENDS (minor).
  6. Lights Out — "holds"; the note itself concerns Oboler's anti-Nazi work. Minor BENDS by the brief's own list (Nazis).
  7. Green Hornet — "holds"; the note concerns Japan's alignment and a "1941 wartime change" of Kato's nationality. Touches foreign policy/Japan; and the 1941 date is not supported by the cited page (Wikipedia's Filipino passage is about a 1989 comics retcon). Mark BENDS (minor) and treat the date as unverified.
  8. Scientific Crime Detection Laboratory, Wingfoot — "holds" with WWI notes. Both notes touch the Great War (Goddard's Ordnance service; Goodyear's wartime blimp programme). Minor BENDS; the entries' labels undersell their own notes.
  9. Compton's cosmic-ray survey — "holds". Stations in British India/Ceylon, Dutch Java, Italian Eritrea, South Africa: the colonial map differs in a Germany-won world with Britain/France selling colonies. Minor BENDS.
  10. Radium Dial (founded 1917) and Tribune/police-radio "Col. McCormick" — "holds". Dial painting boomed on WWI military demand, and McCormick's colonelcy came from WWI service in France (checker's general knowledge, not fetched). Minor; the Southern War could stand in for both — worth a line.
