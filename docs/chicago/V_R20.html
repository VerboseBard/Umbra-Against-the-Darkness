Divergence-label audit

  • Correctly flagged BENDS: Dearborn 1942 halt (WWII); Navy Pier 1918 jail + 1927 "World War I" naming; Welland 1916–19 halt; Anthony Wayne / Harding (the chapter's own cleanest catch — and its substitute, President Leonard Wood, is consistent with the brief's president list); St. Lawrence treaty; Mackinac "World War II halted progress"; the 1943 booklet's "Pearl Harbor"; Fisher Building's WWII asphalt; Chicago Harbor Lock's "the decree's Chief Justice BENDS" (Taft, a Harding appointee — right).   • Presidents: Roosevelt, Hoover, Ickes, PWA, WPA, CCC are all inside the brief's "domestic calendar holds". Harding appears only as a real-record fact under a BENDS label. Coolidge never appears.
  • Problems found: none. One style caution: the Harbor Lock hook says "The Kabbalahs' legend (see R09) says the reversal was a warding" — that presents an R09 proposal as if it were an existing legend. Harmless as a cross-reference, but the owner should know it is a hook citing a hook.
  • "canon": grep shows the word only inside the format label "(proposal, not canon)". No hook calls itself canon.
