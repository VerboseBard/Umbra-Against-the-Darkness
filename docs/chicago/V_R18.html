V_R18 — Fact-check of R18 "The investigator's toolkit"

  • Chapter checked: R18_investigators_toolkit.md (Opus 5.5, 2026-09-26)
  • Checker: Fable 5.1, 2026-09-26
  • Method: WebSearch was exhausted, so every check was done by WebFetch on the chapter's own cited URLs plus a few corroborating pages (Cornell LII statute notes, Wikipedia's 1962 execution list, the Peter Weinberger case, archive.org metadata), and by downloading the full OCR text of the three archive.org primary sources — the 1929 Illinois Crime Survey, the 1939 WPA Illinois guide and the 1933 fair Official Guide Book — and grepping them for every quoted figure. "Survey" below = the Illinois Crime Survey text.
  • Scope: 90 claims (the whole Top 10, every named price, every Survey figure, the federal-agency entries, the gear entries, the divergence labels). Verdict key: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED (right fact + source) / UNSUPPORTED / WRONG.

On facts alone (excluding the five faction-label rows): 10 of 85 = 11.8 %, and six of those ten are small precisions (a first name, a spelling, a date nuance). One genuine howler: the 24-hour kidnapping presumption. Divergence problems

  1. Faction conflation (the mid-run brief correction): six places in R18 put the Secret Service under K. Edgar Hoover or put Section 13 inside the Treasury (rows 86–90). All fixed in place. The Moran entry now reads "holds": the real Moran–Hoover jurisdiction feud is a gift to the setting's Secret Service–Section 13 rivalry rather than something that bends.
  2. Everything else labelled correctly. The WWI/Germany flags are sound: the BAR (1918 war → Southern War proposal), the German flashbulb (1929), the Magnetophon (no 1945 capture), the Seeandbee's 1942 Navy conversion, and the light aviation-surplus flag. Hoover 1929–33 and Roosevelt 1933– are correctly marked as holding. The Mellon-under-Dawes point is correctly left UNVERIFIED for the setting. Nothing about the presidents after Wilson, the empire or foreign policy is mislabelled "holds".
  3. No hook calls itself canon — every hook carries the brief's "(proposal, not canon)" label and the word appears nowhere else (grep-checked).
