1. Start here

The sweep found what the owner asked for, in his own register: about 1,150 dated, named, sourced entries on Chicago and the Great Lakes ring, 1925–1940, with the exact detail a GM steals (an address, a count, a date, a line somebody said), each carrying a divergence check against the 1916 break and a hook labelled proposal. The three biggest surprises: (1) the calendar already loves the alpha — Mother Cabrini died in a Lincoln Park hospital room on 22 December 1917 and the hollow-earth prophet Koresh died on 22 December 1908 with followers waiting for him to rise, the Earling exorcism's last session ended 23 December 1928, and Pacelli, the future pope, was flying the Great Lakes six weeks before the alpha's December (8 Oct–6 Nov 1936), with Mundelein as his Chicago host; (2) Germany's answer to "what did Germany send to the fair" is officially nothing — Chicago's Germans built their own hall and flew the Kaiser's flag to keep the swastika off it, which in the setting is simply the flag of the winning power, so the real 1933 is a rehearsal for the setting's Germany; (3) the Golden Circle's own Chicago history is real and physical — the 1864 Camp Douglas plot (106 arrests on the night of 6–7 November), 4,000–6,000 Confederate dead in concentric trenches at Oak Woods, and a convention hall (the Coliseum) built out of a Confederate prison's walls, where in this setting Leonard Wood was made president. Use the library by thread, not by chapter: §3 knits entries from several lanes into fifteen setting threads, §4 is the register of everything that bends and what to put in its place, §5 is what the research says about material already in the game, and §6 is the short list only the owner can settle.
