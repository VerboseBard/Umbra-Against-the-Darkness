# Umbra Against the Darkness

## Research Wiki

**[Open the research wiki](https://verbosebard.github.io/Umbra-Against-the-Darkness/)**

A chronological historical reference for Umbra Against the Darkness, with **3,651 research entries**, **180 source and review documents**, and **12 archived visual/document assets**. Browse Chicago and the Great Lakes, Mexico, Cuba and the Caribbean. Entries include events, people, places, conditions and folklore; some records overlap.

Share the website link with anyone who wants to read. No account or invitation is needed. Repository editing access is separate and remains controlled by the owner. The wiki defaults to dark mode; the header button switches to light and remembers the choice in your browser.

Search the index, narrow it by dates or subject, and open an entry for its synopsis, sources, maps, available images and research notes. Optional supernatural suggestions appear at the bottom.

### Research, not book canon

Historical evidence, uncertainties, folklore and fictional suggestions retain their separate labels. Nothing here automatically enters a book or establishes a campaign storyline. Only an explicit instruction from the owner to incorporate selected material does that. Publication does not adopt the suggestions.

The public edition retains all indexed entries. Four internal briefing, private folder-inventory and session-dialogue documents stay in the local research archive. Machine-specific paths and local-file links have been removed from this publication copy. Book manuscripts, private source-project history and internal QA files are not part of this repository.

### Sources and images

This is a working research collection, not an assurance that every historical claim or cited video has been independently reviewed. Entry evidence labels, uncertainty notes and source reviews describe the coverage. Map links are modern locators, not proof of period coordinates. Some external archives may require a subscription.

Images and scans are credited in each entry and in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md). Individual asset licenses and rights statements continue to apply. No blanket open-source or Creative Commons license is granted to the owner's original writing or game material.

### Download and maintenance

Use GitHub's **Code → Download ZIP**, extract it, and open `index.html` for a local copy. Source and map links need internet access. `data/wiki-data.json` is the inspectable catalog; `data/wiki-data.js` loads the same records into the site.

This is a static website published from the root of the `main` branch with GitHub Pages. Future published changes should be prepared in the separate publication checkout, reviewed and uploaded only when the owner requests that update. The original local research collection remains the working source.
